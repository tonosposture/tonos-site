Common questions

The scan can’t find me

Prop the phone upright at roughly hip height, about two metres away, with your whole body in frame and even light behind the phone rather than behind you. Wear fitted clothing. Or tap Skip and use the self-check; the report is the same.

The app says to seek care and won’t continue

A safety answer that needs a clinician blocks routines for seven days by design. If you ticked something by mistake, delete and reinstall the app; nothing is stored anywhere else, so this is a clean reset.

I subscribed but the routine is locked

Open the paywall and tap “Restore purchases.” Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tonos Pro → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my data?

Open the calendar from the date on Today, then “Start over,” or delete the app. Everything lives on your phone, so either erases it completely.

An exercise hurts

Tap “This hurts” during the session. Tonos removes it from the rest of your week and never offers it again. Sharp, pinching, or electric pain is a reason to stop and check with a clinician.
