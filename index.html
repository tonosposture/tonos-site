For iPhone

Stand tall. Feel ready.

Tonos measures your posture from two photos, processed on your iPhone, and builds a ten-minute daily routine from what it finds. Re-scan in four weeks and see what changed.

No account. No server. Nothing leaves your phone.

What it does

  • Measure. Apple’s on-device pose detection reads your head angle, shoulder position, and shoulder and hip tilt. Or skip the camera and use the self-check.
  • Report. A posture score out of 100, explained in plain language, with a PDF you can keep.
  • Routine. Ten to fifteen minutes a day from a library of 66 exercises, ramping across the week, hands-free.
  • Progress. Re-scan every four weeks and compare, number by number.
